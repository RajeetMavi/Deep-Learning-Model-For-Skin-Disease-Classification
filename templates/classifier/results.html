Understanding the Visual Explanation

How to interpret Grad-CAM heatmaps for skin lesion classification

The Grad-CAM (Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping) visualization above reveals which visual regions within the image most strongly guided the model toward predicting {{ result.predicted_short }} ({{ result.predicted_prob_pct }}%).

By computing gradients of the predicted class score with respect to the final convolutional feature maps ({{ gradcam.target_layer|default:'Conv_1' }} in MobileNetV2), Grad-CAM calculates importance weights for each spatial feature:

  • Warm colors (red, orange, yellow): Highlight areas containing features that contributed most heavily and positively to the model's classification.
  • Cool colors (cyan, blue): Indicate areas that had little to no positive influence on the selected prediction.
⚠️
Important Medical Notice: Highlighted regions indicate visual areas contributing to the model's prediction based on learned training patterns—they are not a definitive explanation or confirmation of a medical condition. This is an educational prototype and must not be used for diagnosis or clinical decision-making. Always consult a qualified dermatologist for skin examinations.
